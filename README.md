## 🤝 How we work

1. **Tell us your ideas:** send us what you need with all the details in your mind, be it big or small.
2. **We plan it:** we'll contact you to agree on the scope of the project, its features, and the timeline.
3. **We build it:** while we build the project, you'll be sent updates along the way so you can give us feedback if you find something you want to improve.
4. **We deliver:** a finished product for you, ready to use and ready to deploy.
